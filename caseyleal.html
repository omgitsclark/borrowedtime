About Casey Leal

Casey Leal is the newest addition to the Borrowed Time family, bringing bold American traditional work packed with saturated color alongside fine-line botanical and lettering pieces. From creatures and skulls to florals with serious detail, Casey brings versatility and a steady hand to every design.

Specializes In

  • American Traditional - Bold linework with vivid, saturated color
  • Creature & Animal Design - From roaring gorillas to Japanese-influenced motifs
  • Fine-Line Florals & Lettering - Delicate botanical work and custom script
